Today we posted a [preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.15.751855) describing work led by Caroline Kikawa and Andrew Butler using sequencing-based neutralization assays to characterize the human neutralizing antibody landscape to influenza virus.
Our results underscore the impact of the H1N1 HA mutation G155E on a subset of the human population.

---

This preprint is the third installation in our efforts to use sequencing-based neutralization assays to characterize the human neutralization antibody landscape in near real time to inform vaccine strain selection.

We found that certain strains had reduced neutralization by human sera, with the most profound effect that the G155E mutation in H1N1 HA reduced neutralization especially by sera from younger individuals.

## Postscript

A finding of our study was that the emerging G155E mutation in H1N1 caused a strong reduction in neutralization especially among young adults.
